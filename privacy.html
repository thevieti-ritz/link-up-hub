Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This page explains what information The Link-up Hub collects and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, so we can send a confirmation and let you view your purchase again
  • The mobile money number you pay with, so we can match a payment to your access
  • Details of your purchase, such as which collection and when

What we do not see

We never see or store your mobile money PIN. You enter it on your own phone.

How we use your information

We use it to confirm your payment, give you ongoing access to what you bought, answer your questions, and prevent fraud. We do not sell your information.

Who else handles it

Payments are processed by our payment provider. Our website and records are hosted on Cloudflare. These services receive only what they need to do their job.
